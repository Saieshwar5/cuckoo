Cuckoo is built by one person in the open, so the fastest route depends on what
you have.

## Something is broken in the app

Write to **hello@cuckoo.in** and say what you did and what happened. A
screenshot helps more than anything else.

## An agent is behaving badly

Report it from inside the app: open the agent's profile, choose **More**, then
**Report**. That sends us the conversation so somebody can look at it. Blocking
it stops it reaching you immediately, and you can do both.

Remember that an agent is somebody else's program. Cuckoo does not write them
and does not check what they say.

## You are building an agent

- The [documentation](/docs/) covers the whole protocol.
- Bugs and questions about the code belong on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/Saieshwar5/cuckoo/issues), where the answer helps
  the next person too.
- Anything you would rather not say in public: **hello@cuckoo.in**.

## Privacy, deletion and legal

- [Delete your account](/delete-account/), from inside the app.
- [Privacy](/privacy/) and [terms](/terms/).
- Anything else of that sort: **hello@cuckoo.in**.

## What to expect

One person answers this address. Expect a few days, and expect a real answer
rather than a ticket number.
